\assumptionrow{4}{Offline prediction}{Cross-view segmentation can happen forward and backward in time.}

\begin{frame}[t]
\begin{center}
{\large Stage 1: Source Frame Selection}
\end{center}
\vfill
  \begin{center}
  \includegraphics[width=0.9\textwidth]{imgs/ch3/block-1-focus.png}
  \end{center}
  \textcolor[RGB]{155,81,246}{Visibility Scorer} computes score $s_\tau$:
  \[
    s_\tau = 0.99\,a_\tau + 0.01\,c_\tau, \qquad
    a_\tau = \frac{A_\tau}{H \cdot W}, \qquad
    c_\tau =
    1 -
    \frac{\sqrt{\left(c_{y,\tau} - \frac{H}{2}\right)^2 +
    \left(c_{x,\tau} - \frac{W}{2}\right)^2}
    }{\sqrt{\left(\frac{H}{2}\right)^2 +
    \left(\frac{W}{2}\right)^2}},
  \]
  \hspace{4mm} where $a_\tau$ is the normalised mask area and $c_\tau$ is the mask's centrality.
\vfill
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}[t]
\begin{center}
{\large Cropping the input is ineffective}
\end{center}
\vfill
\begin{center}
\small
\setlength{\tabcolsep}{10pt}
\begin{tabular}{lc}
  \toprule
  What the descriptor-VLM sees & IoU (\textit{Ego2Exo}) \\
  \midrule
  Raw frame + mask overlay & 41.2 \\
  Raw + overlay + crop     & 41.1 \\
  Only tight crop          & 18.0 \\
  \bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\vspace{0.8em}
\begin{center}
\begin{minipage}{0.88\textwidth}
\small
Replacing the full frame with a tight crop of the object more than halves IoU, from 41.1 to 18.0. The identity of a small object is carried by its surroundings, so a crop that maximises the object's pixels destroys the very evidence the VLM uses to name it. Appending a zoom alongside the full frame instead neither helps nor harms.
\end{minipage}
\end{center}
\vfill
\end{frame}

\[
  \hat{M}^D_i =
  \begin{cases}
    \hat{M}^{\rightarrow}_i, & a^\ast(i) \leq i,\\[3pt]
    \hat{M}^{\leftarrow}_i, & a^\ast(i) > i,
  \end{cases}
  \qquad
  a^\ast(i) = \operatorname*{arg\,min}_{a \in \mathcal{A}} \lvert i - a \rvert
\]
\end{center}
\vspace{0.4em}
\begin{center}
\begin{minipage}{0.88\textwidth}
\small
SAM~2 propagates the anchor masks forward from the earliest anchor and backward from the latest, both passes carrying every anchor. For each frame the pipeline keeps the prediction from the pass whose nearest anchor $a^\ast(i)$ is closer, so the chain separating a frame from the anchor it leans on stays short. This minimises accumulated drift in the long gaps between anchors.
\end{minipage}
\end{center}
\vfill
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}[t]
\begin{center}
{\large Evaluation metrics}
\end{center}
\vfill
\begin{center}
\[
  \text{LE} = \frac{\lVert \varsigma(\hat{M}) - \varsigma(M) \rVert_2}{d},
  \qquad d = \sqrt{H^2 + W^2},
  \qquad
  \text{CA} = \frac{2 P R}{P + R}
\]
\end{center}
\vspace{0.4em}
\begin{center}
\begin{minipage}{0.88\textwidth}
\small
Intersection over Union is the primary metric, averaged over every evaluated destination frame of a take. Location Error is the distance between predicted and ground-truth mask centroids $\varsigma(\cdot)$, normalised by the image diagonal $d$, so it isolates placement regardless of shape. Contour Accuracy is the boundary F-measure of precision $P$ and recall $R$, isolating outline quality.
\end{minipage}
\end{center}
\vfill
\end{frame}
